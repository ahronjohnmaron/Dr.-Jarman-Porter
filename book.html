The Book

After the Bell Rings

Before Class Begins — A Common Sense Approach to Everyday Educational Issues

This is not a research study. It is not a policy paper.

This book is about what happens in the space between the bells — in the bedroom the night before, on the bus, in the two minutes at the locker, in a text message at 11:47 p.m.

Written for parents, teachers, administrators and young people, it offers practical, honest guidance drawn from more than twenty-five years at the door, in the cafeteria and in the hallway.
